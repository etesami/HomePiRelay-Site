HomePiRelay

A private, single-household home automation relay.

HomePiRelay is a small personal application that runs on a Raspberry Pi in the owner's home. It relays home events, such as messages and camera notifications, to the owner's own private chat, and makes the owner's own cameras viewable on their home network.

Who it is for

HomePiRelay is not a public service. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own devices and accounts. There is no sign-up and no other users.

Google Nest integration

With the owner's permission, HomePiRelay uses the Google Smart Device Management API to:

  • view live video from the owner's own Nest cameras on the home network;
  • receive camera events (motion, person) and event snapshots, to notify the owner.

All data stays on the owner's own device. See the Privacy Policy.
